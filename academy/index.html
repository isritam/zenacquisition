How is this different from hiring an agency? +

An agency runs your pipeline for you, every month, for as long as you keep paying. This is a one-time, 90-day training program. We teach your team to run the system so your business can own the process internally.

How quickly do we start seeing results? +

Most teams start seeing qualified calls within the first 7–10 business days of implementing what they're taught. The full system ramps up over the 90-day implementation period.

What do I need to have ready before starting? +

A proven offer that has closed at least one paying client, someone on your team who can own outbound, and a calendar link. We teach your team the acquisition process, targeting, messaging, sequences, and execution.

Do you run ads? +

No. The core system focuses on organic outbound through channels such as cold email, LinkedIn, and calling.

What industries do you work with? +

Primarily coaches and agency owners in India, including business coaching, marketing agencies, consulting, and professional services. If you have a high-ticket offer and a team willing to be trained, the program may be a fit.

What happens on the enrollment call? +

We'll spend 30–45 minutes understanding your offer, pipeline, and team. We'll walk through the 90-day program, explain how the guarantee works, and determine whether the program is the right fit.
